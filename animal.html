Forest Animals

Forests are home to many animals, including tigers, elephants, monkeys, deer and colourful birds. These animals live among trees and plants.

Ocean Animals

Oceans are home to amazing creatures such as dolphins, whales, sharks, sea turtles and colourful fish. They live in salt water.

Desert Animals

Deserts are dry habitats where animals such as camels, snakes, lizards and desert foxes survive with very little water.
