Jungle Wildlife

Jungles are home to many fascinating animals. Tigers, elephants, monkeys, parrots and snakes live among the trees and plants.

Amazing Jungle Plants

Jungles contain tall trees, colourful flowers, thick vines and giant leaves. These plants provide food and shelter for many animals.
